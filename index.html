About me

The
Designer
Behind
the Work.

I've been fascinated by videogames since I was young — from the rooftops of Assassin's Creed Brotherhood to the depths of Subnautica. Understanding the mechanics behind that feeling is what led me to game design.

"The most powerful moments in games are the ones where you understand something without being told — where the rule and the meaning arrive at the same time."

My passion is mechanic design: how to tell a story through player actions. I'm also drawn to educational games, where the challenge is making something that genuinely teaches without sacrificing game feel.

Currently Lead Designer & Producer at Wild Alchemists, working on Hot Potato — Audience Award winner at Match in Games 2025. Based in Madrid.

Outside of games: music, reading, and sport.

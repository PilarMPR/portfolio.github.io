About me

The
Designer
Behind
the Work.

I've been fascinated by videogames since I was young — from the rooftops of Assassin's Creed Brotherhood to the depths of Subnautica. Understanding the mechanics behind that feeling is what led me to game design.

My passion is mechanic design — specifically, how to tell a story through player actions. The most powerful moments in games are the ones where you understand something without being told.

I'm also drawn to educational games. Players don't care about your learning objectives — the game has to earn their attention first. Getting that balance right is one of the most interesting problems I know.

Currently Lead Designer & Producer at Wild Alchemists, working on Hot Potato — Audience Award winner at Match in Games 2025. Based in Madrid.

Context

What is Healthy Jeart?

An educational platformer that repurposed F2P monetisation mechanics — currencies, shops, engagement loops — to reward real-world healthy habits in children aged 8–14, instead of extracting money.

"Can the same mechanics that drive compulsive spending drive behavioural change instead? That was the premise we tested."
How it works

Core System

Play game→Log real habit→Earn hard currency→Unlock content→Share community

Hard currency — normally reserved for real-money purchases — was earned by completing health questionnaires and participating in a community forum divided by age range. The inversion of the standard F2P model was the key design insight.

My responsibilities

What I Contributed

  • Designed and balanced mechanics navigating the tension between engagement and educational value.
  • Developed items and power-ups reinforcing healthy/unhealthy distinctions with positive game feel.
  • Designed interface mock-ups and menu flow in Adobe XD.
  • Contributed to challenge and prize systems for player retention.
  • Conducted iterative playtesting to refine difficulty, pacing, and player experience.
Key decisions & learnings

Challenges

Making habit-tracking feel rewarding rather than a chore. If logging a habit felt like homework, players would abandon it. Significant iteration went into feedback quality — how the game responds to the action, not just that it rewards it.

Working as an intern inside an established senior team taught me to make meaningful contributions within pre-existing systems, and to advocate for design ideas without controlling the creative direction.
